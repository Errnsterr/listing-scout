# Listing Scout: disclaimer (please read)

Listing Scout helps you spot rentals and homes faster. It's a helper, not a professional.

### What it is not

- Not a real estate agent or broker. Not a legal adviser. This page is not legal advice.
- Not financial advice. Prices and "days on market" come from listing sites.
- It never emails, calls or messages landlords, property managers or agents for you.

### Apply for me

- Your bot fills in the listing's own application and submits it only after your yes for that place. Nothing goes out without your yes.
- You are responsible for checking that every answer is true and correct before you say yes.
- Application fees are set by the landlord or property manager, and are often not refundable.
- Approval is up to the landlord. Applying doesn't mean you'll get the place.

### About the listings

- Listings come from other sites and property managers. They can be wrong, out of date, or already rented.
- The bot never makes up details. If something isn't listed, it says "not listed yet." It still can't guarantee what a site shows.

### Stay safe

- See a place in person, or have someone you trust see it, before you sign anything.
- Never send money or a deposit before you've seen the place. Pay an application fee only on the listing's own apply page, and only if you trust it.
- When the bot spots warning signs (a price far below similar places, odd payment wording, a photo that doesn't match the address), it says "See it before paying anything." Take that seriously.

### Listing sites

- You're responsible for following each listing site's terms of use, including saved-search and alert settings.

### Texting

- Texting is opt-in: each person texts the bot first.
- Text STOP anytime and the texts stop right away.
- Message and data rates may apply.

### Fair housing

- Filters are only for features of the home (beds, baths, price, garage, layout, pets). Never use them to pick or exclude people based on who they are or who lives there.

### Who it's for

- For US rentals, and home buying (experimental).
- Buying: not financial, mortgage or investment advice. Prices, HOA dues and "days on market" come from listing sites. Talk to a licensed agent, lender or adviser before you make an offer.

  It never contacts listing agents or sellers for you.

- For adults 18 and over.
- Support is best effort, with no guarantees.

**Fair housing:** Listing Scout only filters on listing facts you choose (price, beds, place, pets), never on who lives in an area, and it does not repeat discriminatory listing wording.
