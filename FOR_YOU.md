# Listing Scout: what you do

Your bot does almost all of it.
These are the only steps that need you.
Plan on about 60 to 90 minutes.
The first real alert can take up to a day.

## Good to know first
- Your phone and laptop can be off. The scout runs on your bot's own computer.
- If that computer restarts, the scout starts again on its own.
- If alerts stop, text Help or ask your bot.
- You may need to solve a captcha ("I'm not a robot") once or twice.
- It is for US rentals. Buying a home also works, but it is still new.
- You must be 18 or older.

## Your steps
1. Tell your bot what you want: the area, beds, price, pets and move-in date.
2. Say your time zone when the bot asks.
3. Say yes to the alerts email name the bot picks. You can't change it later.
4. Make a Photon account. Go to app.photon.codes. Choose Continue with Google.
5. Make a project called Listing Scout. Open Configure. Click Reveal next to Project secret.
6. Paste the secret into the private box your bot sends. Never paste it in chat.
7. On Photon's Users page, add your mobile number.
8. Tap the contact card your bot sends. Save it.
9. Text "hi" to that contact.
10. Look at the sample alert. Say yes if you like it.
11. Pick a plan when the bot asks. Say yes to go live.
12. If a site shows a captcha, solve it.

## If you get stuck
- Tell your bot what you see on the screen. A screenshot is fine.
- If Photon asks you to pay or show ID, stop. Tell your bot first.
- If a site won't let your bot sign up, make the account yourself. Then forward its alert emails to your bot's alerts email.

## Adding someone
1. Ask your bot: "add my partner".
2. Add their mobile number on Photon's Users page.
3. Forward the invite text your bot sends you.

## When you're done
1. Text "end my search". Reply yes.
2. Say if you want your data deleted.
3. On Photon's Users page, remove each person.

Text STOP anytime to stop all texts.
