# Listing Scout: what you do

Your bot does almost all of it.

These are the only steps that need you.

Plan on about an hour.

You'll see your first matches in about 10 minutes.

The first real alert can take up to a day.

When you find a place, your bot can fill in the application for you.

Nothing goes out without your yes.

## Good to know first

- Your phone and laptop can be off. The scout runs on your bot's own computer.
- If that computer restarts, the scout starts again on its own.
- If alerts stop, text Help or ask your bot.
- You may need to solve a captcha ("I'm not a robot") once or twice.
- It is for US rentals. Buying a home also works, but it is still new.
- You must be 18 or older.
- Everyone's welcome text says staying in means they're 18+, and how to text STOP.
- Your bot never emails or calls landlords.
- It applies only after you say yes for that place.

## Your steps

1. Say renting or buying. Then answer 4 quick questions: where, beds and baths, budget (comfortable and stretch), and move date.
2. Look over the matches your bot shows you. Then set up texts and listing-site alerts.
3. Say yes to the alerts email name the bot picks. You can't change it later.
4. Make a Photon account. Go to app.photon.codes. Choose Continue with Google.
5. Make a project called Listing Scout. Open Configure. Click Reveal next to Project secret.
6. Paste the secret into the private box your bot sends. Never paste it in chat.
7. On Photon's Users page, add your mobile number.
8. Tap the contact card your bot sends. Save it.
9. Text "hi" to that contact.
10. Look at the sample alert. Say yes if you like it.
11. Pick a plan when the bot asks. Say yes to go live.
12. If a site shows a captcha, solve it.

## If you get stuck

- Tell your bot what you see on the screen. A screenshot is fine.
- If Photon asks you to pay or show ID, stop. Tell your bot first.
- If a site won't let your bot sign up, make the account yourself. Then forward its alert emails to your bot's alerts email.

## Adding someone

1. Ask your bot: "add my partner".
2. Add their mobile number on Photon's Users page.
3. Forward the invite text your bot sends you.

## When you're done

1. Text "end my search", then text yes.
2. Say if you want your data deleted.
3. On Photon's Users page, remove each person.

Text STOP anytime to stop all texts.

## Apply for me

Your bot can fill in a rental application for you.

Nothing goes out without your yes.

It never emails or calls the landlord.

### Set it up once

1. Tell your bot: "set up my application profile".
2. Answer its questions: your job, income, past homes and references, move date, who will live with you, pets and cars.
3. Your name, address, phone, email and card come from your bot's vault.

### Each time you apply

1. Text "apply" on a place you like. For the second place in a text, text "apply 2".
2. Open the Grok Bot app. Your bot shows the place and what it needs.
3. Say yes to start.
4. Give your bot the things below when it asks.
5. Check what it filled in and the fee. Say yes to send it.

### What you must give each time

1. **Social Security number and date of birth.** Type them into the private form your bot sends. They are used once and never saved.
2. **Photo ID or pay stubs.** Attach them in the Grok Bot app.
3. **ID selfie checks.** If the site asks for a selfie or a scan of your license, you do it.
4. **Credit check okay and your signature.** Only you can agree and sign.
5. **Phone codes.** Tell your bot the code the site texts you.

If a listing looks like a scam, your bot stops and asks you first.

Fees are set by the landlord and are often not refundable.

On a shared search, each person uses their own profile.

## Later, one at a time

Your bot asks about pets, must-haves, anyone searching with you (a partner, roommate, family, friend or coworker) and Apply for me. It shows the property managers it already watches. Skip anything.

It reminds you in chat, never by text, and stops after two skips. Say "add pets" (or any detail) anytime.
